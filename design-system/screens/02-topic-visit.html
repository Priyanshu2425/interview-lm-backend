Attention Mechanisms, Transformers & BERT
Module 6 · Natural Language Processing
Question

In scaled dot-product attention, why is QKᵀ divided by √dk? Talk me through what goes wrong if you leave it out.

Your answer

It normalises the scores so the attention weights sum to one, so the softmax behaves properly across the sequence.

Probing

The softmax already guarantees the weights sum to one, with or without the divisor. So that can't be what the scaling is for. Try again: what happens to the size of those dot products as dk grows?

Your answer

They get bigger, I think, because you're summing over more dimensions. So the numbers going into softmax get large.

Hint

Right. Now finish the chain. Large inputs to softmax, then what? Think about the shape of the softmax curve out in its tails, and what that does to the gradient during training.
